Sportskeeda | NFL Draft strategy

Turning an untapped, high-CPM audience opportunity into a repeatable growth model

I identified the NFL Draft as a search-led audience opportunity months before the event, then built the operating plan around content architecture, resource allocation, SEO tracking, and readiness before demand spiked.

What I owned

I built the content strategy from scratch for a small NFL vertical and turned the Draft into a planned organic growth opportunity.

Key decisions

I prioritised the top 300 searched prospects, built pillar pages with 5-6 supporting articles, and optimised for long-tail demand and Google Discover.

Systems built

I created daily progress tracking, weekly SERP tracking, interlinking structures, and real-time ranking and pageview review rhythms.

Team leverage

I scaled the operating team from myself plus two writers to one assistant content manager, eight writers, two editors, and one SEO analyst.

Output system

1,500 articles prepared across prospect-focused pillar and support content.

Revenue

INR 3-4 lakh during the four Draft days and INR 50 lakh annual revenue contribution.

Audience

Sessions path: 108K Jun-Jul monthly sessions to 250K Aug-Sep season-start sessions to 550K Jan-Feb peak sessions.

Scale effect
